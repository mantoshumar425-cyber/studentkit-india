What can you contact us about?

You can contact StudentKit India if you notice an error in a tool, find incorrect information, have a suggestion for a new student tool, or want to provide feedback about the website.

Please do not send passwords, payment card information, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information through email.

We aim to review legitimate inquiries and respond when appropriate. Response times may vary depending on the nature and volume of messages received.
