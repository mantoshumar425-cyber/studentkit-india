Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

StudentKit India ("StudentKit", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used.

1. Information We Collect

StudentKit India is primarily designed to provide browser-based educational tools and resources. We do not require users to create an account to use the basic tools available on the website.

Some tools may process information entered by a user directly inside the browser. For example, text entered into the Word Counter or numbers entered into the Percentage Calculator may be processed locally to provide the requested result.

2. Information You Provide

If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive information included in your message, such as your name, email address and the contents of your communication.

Please do not send passwords, payment information or other confidential information through ordinary contact messages.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, StudentKit India may receive limited technical information through hosting providers, analytics services, advertising services or standard web technologies.

This information may include browser type, device information, approximate location, referring pages, pages visited and general usage information. Such information may be used for security, performance, analytics and website improvement.

4. Cookies

StudentKit India and third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, understand website usage, improve services or display relevant advertising.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

StudentKit India may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to display advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide advertisements based on a user's visits to this website and other websites.

Google uses advertising technologies to help deliver and measure advertisements. Users may have options to manage advertising personalization through Google's advertising settings and applicable privacy controls.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, security, fonts or other website functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children's Privacy

StudentKit India is intended to provide general educational tools and resources. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted to us unnecessarily, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. External Links

StudentKit India may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites operated by third parties.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the services and technologies used, you may be able to control cookies, browser permissions and advertising preferences through your browser or the relevant third-party service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or StudentKit India's privacy practices, please contact the website owner through the contact method provided on the website.
