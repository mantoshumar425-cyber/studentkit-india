CALCULATION GUIDE

How to calculate percentage from marks

Percentage tells you how many marks you obtained compared with the total marks available. It is commonly used by students to understand their examination performance.

Percentage formula

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

For example, if a student obtains 425 marks out of a total of 500 marks, the calculation is:

(425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

You can use the StudentKit percentage calculator above to perform the same calculation instantly.

STUDY

How to make an effective study plan

A good study plan divides available time between learning new topics, revision, practice and rest. Start by listing your subjects and upcoming exams. Then divide larger chapters into smaller study sessions that are easier to complete.

Regular revision is important because learning a chapter once does not guarantee that you will remember it during an examination. Keep some time every week for reviewing previously studied topics.

EXAMS

How to prepare for an examination

Examination preparation becomes easier when students understand their syllabus and divide it into manageable sections. Begin with concepts, then solve examples and practice questions.

Before the examination, revise important formulas, definitions, diagrams and commonly tested concepts. Practice writing answers within the available time so that you become comfortable with the examination format.

PRODUCTIVITY

Simple ways to improve study productivity

Studying for long hours is not always the same as studying effectively. A focused session with a clear goal can be more useful than sitting with books for a long period without a plan.

Keep your study area organised, remove unnecessary distractions and decide what you want to complete before beginning a session. After studying, briefly review what you learned.
