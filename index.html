LEARN THE METHOD

Percentage calculation, explained simply.

Percentage compares a student's obtained marks with the total marks available. It is one of the most common calculations used in school examinations.

Percentage formula

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

For example, 425 marks out of 500 gives:

(425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%
